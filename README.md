## About This Blog

This blog explores healthcare-associated infections in Germany and explains why a relatively low hospital infection rate does not always mean a low national burden.

The blog is adapted from my Assignment 2 report and uses simplified explanations, visualisations, and a summary table to communicate the main findings.

## Intended Audience

This blog is written for general readers who are interested in health and healthcare but may not have a background in medical research or statistics.
